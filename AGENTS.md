# AhaGuru MCQ Reasoning Assistant

## Overview
This application is a specialized validation tool for AhaGuru mentors and directors to assess the quality of AI-generated reasoning for multiple-choice questions.

## How to use
1. **Browse Questions**: Scroll through the list of MCQs.
2. **Compare Reasonings**: Click on any option (A, B, C, or D) to view and compare how different AI models explained that specific choice.
3. **Vote**: For each question, select the model that provided the most accurate and pedagogically sound reasoning.
4. **Review Results**: Click the "Check Your Vote" button at the top/bottom to see a summary of your selections.

## Persistence
Votes are saved to your browser's local storage automatically.
